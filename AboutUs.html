Our Background



Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a fictional small bakery based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The bakery sells freshly baked bread, pastries, cupcakes, cookies and customised cakes for birthdays and special occasions. Its aim is to provide good quality baked products at affordable prices while giving customers friendly service


Our Mission and Goal



Our customers are our main priority. We put them first in every situation. Our mission is to put our industry to the map and put our name at the top and by doing that we have to do to meet the means of our customers. So our goal is to increase Sweet Crumbs Bakery's online visibility and make it easier for customers to enquire about its products. The more cakes and everything we are able to sell to our customers in our nation, the more we spread our products to other nations. We always keep in mind that The Customer Is Always Right.
